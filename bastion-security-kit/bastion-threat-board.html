BASTION SECURITY THREAT BOARD

Five repeat categories, filed by the Shift Lead. Every incident that lands on a Tier-1 desk traces back to one of these. Know the motive before you know the malware.

FILED

Script Kiddie

AKA: Low-Skill Opportunist

MOTIVE: BRAGGING RIGHTS
MO
Downloads a tool someone else built, points it at a target for curiosity or clout — no real strategy behind it.
Tools of the trade
Public exploit kits, DDoS booter services, defacement scripts
Countermeasure
Basic patching and hardening close most of the doors this actor knows how to open
THREAT LEVEL: NOVICE ●○○○○
NOTE: low sophistication, not low risk — an unpatched system is just as compromised whether the attacker was a genius or downloaded the tool an hour ago.
FILED

Hacktivist

AKA: The Statement-Maker

MOTIVE: POLITICAL / SOCIAL
MO
Wants an audience, not a payout — defaces a site or leaks data to make a point, then often takes credit publicly.
Tools of the trade
Website defacement kits, leaked-data dumps, coordinated DDoS campaigns
Countermeasure
Harden public-facing systems, have an incident-comms plan ready, DDoS mitigation service
THREAT LEVEL: NOVICE–INTERMEDIATE ●●○○○
NOTE: visibility is the goal — a quiet, contained incident is a failed attack from this actor's perspective.
FILED

Organized Crime

AKA: The Business

MOTIVE: MONEY
MO
Runs cybercrime like a company — ransomware-as-a-service, stolen card data resold on dark-web markets, help desks for victims paying ransom.
Tools of the trade
RaaS kits, phishing infrastructure, card skimmers
Countermeasure
Offline-tested backups, endpoint detection and response, security-awareness training
THREAT LEVEL: INTERMEDIATE–ADVANCED ●●●●○
NOTE: well-resourced and patient — treat every payload like it was tested against your specific defenses, because it might have been.
FILED

Nation-State / APT

AKA: The Long Game

MOTIVE: ESPIONAGE / SABOTAGE
MO
Backed by a government, extremely well-funded — gets in quietly and stays for months, gathering intelligence or waiting for the right moment to act.
Tools of the trade
Custom malware, zero-day exploits, supply-chain compromise
Countermeasure
Defense in depth, threat-intel feeds, anomaly-based monitoring over long time windows
THREAT LEVEL: SEVERE ●●●●●
NOTE: dwell time is the tell — the breach you find today may have started months ago. Long, quiet access is the signature, not a smash-and-grab.
FILED

Insider Threat

AKA: Already Has The Keys

MOTIVE: MONEY, REVENGE, OR AN ACCIDENT
MO
No break-in required — a legitimate account, used the wrong way, whether it's deliberate sabotage, quiet data theft, or just a careless mistake.
Tools of the trade
Valid credentials, authorized access, standard company tools
Countermeasure
Least privilege, periodic access reviews, DLP, an off-boarding checklist that actually gets followed
THREAT LEVEL: VARIABLE ●●●○○
NOTE: hardest one to profile — this dossier covers a category, not a signature. The access itself is the risk, malicious or not.
